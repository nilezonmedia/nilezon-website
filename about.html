About Nilezon

One river, many nations

Nilezon is an independent knowledge platform dedicated to the Nile River Basin — its hydrology, engineering, history, geopolitics and shared future across eleven nations.

Our mission

To become the world's most trusted independent resource on the Nile Basin: rigorous, evidence-first, and accountable to sources rather than to any single national narrative. The river connects eleven nations; the knowledge about it should serve all of them.

Our method

Nilezon is built on a simple discipline: every claim is anchored to a verifiable source, and every point of genuine contestation is stated as contested rather than resolved by assertion. Where the data is uncertain, we say so. Where a figure is disputed, we show the dispute.

The NKIS system

Every publication receives a permanent identifier through the Nilezon Knowledge Identification System — a platform ID (NZ-0001) and a category ID (HYD-0001) that never change, so research can be cited with lasting confidence. Browse the full index in the Archive.

Coverage

  • Hydrology — the science of the water itself.
  • Engineering — dams, reservoirs and irrigation.
  • History — the basin's past and the treaties that endure.
  • Geopolitics — sovereignty, security and cooperation.
  • Environment — climate, sediment and the living basin.
