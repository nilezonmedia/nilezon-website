Category · History

A river with memory

The Nile carries more than water. From Aksum and ancient Egypt to the colonial agreements of 1929 and 1959, the basin's past is written into the legal and political arguments of the present. Nilezon reads that history with a prosecutorial eye for the source.

Why it matters

Colonial-era instruments still shape who claims what share of the river. Reading the original texts — and the circumstances that produced them — is essential to understanding the disputes they left behind.

What we cover

  • Ancient waters — Aksum, Egypt and the civilisations the river fed
  • Colonial cartography — how empires drew, and claimed, the basin
  • The treaties that endure — 1902, 1929, 1959 and their long shadow
