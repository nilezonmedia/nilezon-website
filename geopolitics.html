Category · Geopolitics

Sovereignty, drop by drop

Eleven nations share one river, and no question about the Nile is ever only technical. Nilezon maps the geopolitical architecture of the basin — the negotiations, the legal frameworks, and the security logic that turns hydrology into statecraft.

Why it matters

Water security is national security for every riparian state. Following the actual negotiating positions, legal claims and institutional mechanisms — rather than the rhetoric around them — is how Nilezon keeps the analysis honest.

What we cover

  • Riparian rights — upstream and downstream claims under international law
  • GERD diplomacy — the negotiations, the deadlocks and the frameworks
  • Cooperation architecture — the Nile Basin Initiative and the Entebbe agreement
