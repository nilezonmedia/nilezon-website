Building on the river

The Nile is one of the most heavily engineered river systems on Earth. From the Aswan High Dam to the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam, every structure reshapes the water — and the politics — downstream. Nilezon examines how these systems are designed, filled and operated.

Why it matters

A dam is a hydrological instrument before it is a political one. Understanding reservoir volumes, filling schedules, turbine capacity and sediment management is what separates informed analysis from headline noise.

What we cover

  • Storage and filling — reservoir volumes, drought years and release rules
  • Hydropower — installed capacity, firm energy and the regional grid
  • Irrigation and sediment — the downstream ledger of every upstream structure
